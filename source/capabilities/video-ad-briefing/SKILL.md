# Video Ad Briefing

Create a production brief before requesting generation when the user has not
already supplied an approved brief.

## Workflow

1. Capture the campaign goal, offer, audience, placement, duration, aspect
   ratio, brand constraints, required claims, call to action, and available
   source assets.
2. Identify missing information that materially affects the creative. Use
   clearly labeled assumptions for details that can be revised safely.
3. Draft the hook, scene sequence, spoken script, on-screen copy, visual
   direction, audio direction, and end card.
4. Flag factual claims, testimonials, regulated claims, trademarks, likenesses,
   and licensed assets that require evidence or approval.
5. Present the brief for approval. Record an immutable brief revision label so
   generation results can be traced to the approved input.

Keep copy appropriate to the requested duration and placement. Preserve the
user's source language for approved claims. Never represent generated people,
voices, results, or testimonials as authentic evidence.

## Output

Return a concise brief containing:

- campaign and audience;
- format, duration, and placement;
- hook, script, scenes, captions, and call to action;
- asset inputs and brand constraints;
- assumptions, claim evidence, and approval status;
- brief revision label.
