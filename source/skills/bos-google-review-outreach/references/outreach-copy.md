# Review outreach copy policy

Use three distinct neutral templates:

1. Initial request: thank the family and invite them to share their experience.
2. Follow-up: briefly repeat the optional request.
3. Final note: state that this is the final request and include the same
   confirmation link.

Use `{{review_confirmation_url}}`, which opens the BOS confirmation page before
redirecting to Google. Use verified personalization only. Omit incentives,
requested star ratings, sentiment screening, and claims of delivery before a
SendGrid delivery event.
