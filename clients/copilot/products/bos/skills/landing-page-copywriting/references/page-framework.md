# Landing Page Framework

Use the smallest set of sections needed for the visitor's risk and awareness level.

1. **Hero:** Audience-relevant outcome, short explanation of the offer, primary CTA, and strongest proof or risk reducer.
2. **Problem and desired change:** Reflect the visitor's current situation and show the better state without dramatizing it.
3. **Benefits:** Present three to five concrete changes the customer gets. Connect each benefit to the primary desire.
4. **How it works:** Explain the path in three simple steps. Make time, effort, and next actions clear.
5. **Proof:** Place reviews, results, demonstrations, credentials, or comparisons beside the claims they support.
6. **Offer:** State what is included, price or commitment when appropriate, eligibility, timeframe, support, bonuses, and terms.
7. **Risk reversal:** Explain guarantees, cancellation, trial, privacy, or what happens if the offer is a poor fit.
8. **Objections and FAQ:** Answer the few questions that stop a qualified visitor from acting.
9. **Final CTA:** Restate the core outcome and describe the exact next step.

## Hero formula options

- `Get [desired outcome] without [major obstacle].`
- `[Desired outcome] for [audience], with [meaningful differentiator].`
- `[Do the valuable job] in [supported timeframe], even if [real objection].`
- `Feel [emotional outcome] with [plain-language offer or mechanism].`

Use formulas as scaffolding. Replace generic words with the audience's own language.

## Proof hierarchy

Prefer the strongest available proof:

1. Direct, verified outcome evidence
2. Relevant customer stories and testimonials
3. Demonstration or sample
4. Specific process and transparent terms
5. Credentials, independent reviews, or trusted affiliations
6. Honest explanation of what is known and still uncertain

Label missing proof as an internal placeholder. Never publish a fabricated placeholder as a real claim.
