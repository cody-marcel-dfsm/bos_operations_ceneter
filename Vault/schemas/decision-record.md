# Decision record schema

- **Title:** concise decision name
- **Status:** proposed, accepted, superseded, or retired
- **Date:** ISO 8601 date
- **Context:** evidence and constraints that require a decision
- **Decision:** selected project behavior and owning surface
- **Consequences:** expected outcomes and tradeoffs
- **Validation:** tests or evidence that prove the decision
- **Supersedes:** prior decision records, when applicable
