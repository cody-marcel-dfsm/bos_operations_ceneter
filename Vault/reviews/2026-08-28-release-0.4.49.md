## Findings

authority-scoped confirmed-snapshot cache, ordered initialization stage, and

complete release diff, including all previously untracked Vault records,
canonical sources, tests, generated clients, and the 0.4.49 version bump.

## Architecture conclusion

Release 0.4.49 preserves explicit authority, application neutrality,
server-owned canonical state, Router-to-PO-to-GO mutation ownership,
credential containment, fail-closed behavior, deterministic generation, and
customer-owned extension preservation. The remote settings service remains an
independently implemented and reviewed dependency in its owning repository;
the packaged client fails closed until its tools and capabilities are present.
